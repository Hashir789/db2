# Kitaab - Your Personal Accountability Companion

## What is Kitaab?

Kitaab (which means "book" in Arabic) is a personal accountability application designed to help you track your spiritual and moral progress. Think of it as a digital journal where you can record your good deeds (Hasanaat) and areas where you need improvement (Saiyyiaat), helping you stay mindful and accountable in your daily life.

## The Big Picture

### Why Kitaab Exists

In our busy lives, it's easy to lose track of our spiritual and moral development. Kitaab helps you:

- **Stay Accountable**: Record your daily actions and reflect on them
- **Track Progress**: See how you're improving over time
- **Stay Motivated**: Set goals and celebrate achievements
- **Get Support**: Share your journey with trusted friends or mentors
- **Reflect Daily**: Take time each day to think about your actions

### The Philosophy

Kitaab is built on the principle that awareness leads to improvement. By consistently tracking your deeds—both positive and areas for growth—you develop greater self-awareness and can make more intentional choices in your daily life.

## Core Concepts

### 1. Deeds (Hasanaat & Saiyyiaat)

**Hasanaat** are your good deeds—actions, habits, or practices you want to maintain or improve. Examples:
- Daily prayers
- Reading religious texts
- Helping others
- Exercising patience
- Acts of charity

**Saiyyiaat** are areas where you need improvement—habits or behaviors you want to reduce or eliminate. Examples:
- Negative thoughts
- Wasting time
- Being impatient
- Skipping important practices

### 2. Deed Items (The Hierarchy)

Think of your deeds like a filing cabinet with folders and subfolders:

**Level 1 - Main Categories**
- "Daily Prayers"
- "Charity"
- "Self-Improvement"

**Level 2 - Subcategories**
- Under "Daily Prayers": "Fajr", "Dhuhr", "Asr", "Maghrib", "Isha"
- Under "Charity": "Donations", "Volunteering", "Helping Neighbors"

**Level 3 - Specific Actions**
- Under "Fajr": "Prayed on time", "Prayed late", "Missed"

This three-level structure helps you organize your tracking in a way that makes sense to you.

### 3. Entries (Daily Records)

An **entry** is a record of what you did on a specific day for a specific deed item. You can record entries in two ways:

**Scale-Based**: Choose from predefined options
- Example: For "Fajr Prayer", you might have: "Prayed on time", "Prayed late", "Missed"
- Example: For "Quality of Work", you might have: "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", "Poor"

**Count-Based**: Enter a number
- Example: "Number of pages read" = 15
- Example: "Amount donated" = $50.00
- Example: "Minutes of exercise" = 30

### 4. Scales (Measurement Systems)

A **scale** is how you measure a particular deed. You define the options yourself:

**Simple Yes/No Scale:**
- Yes
- No

**Quality Scale:**
- Excellent
- Good
- Fair
- Poor

**Custom Scale:**
- Prayed on time
- Prayed late
- Missed

You can even update your scales over time as your needs change, and old entries will still be valid.

## How It Works

### The Basic Flow

1. **Create Your Deeds**: Set up categories for Hasanaat (good deeds) and Saiyyiaat (areas for improvement)

2. **Organize with Items**: Break down each deed into specific items (up to 3 levels of organization)

3. **Define How to Measure**: For each deed, choose whether to use a scale (like Yes/No or Excellent/Good/Fair) or count numbers

4. **Record Daily**: Each day, log your entries for the items you want to track

5. **Review & Reflect**: Look at your progress over time, write daily reflections, and adjust as needed

### Example: Tracking Daily Prayers

**Step 1**: Create a deed called "Daily Prayers" (Hasanaat)

**Step 2**: Create items:
- Level 1: "Fajr", "Dhuhr", "Asr", "Maghrib", "Isha"

**Step 3**: Create a scale with options:
- "Prayed on time"
- "Prayed late"
- "Missed"

**Step 4**: Each day, record which option applies to each prayer

**Step 5**: Over time, see your patterns—are you improving? Which prayers need more attention?

## Key Features

### 1. Hierarchical Organization

Organize your deeds in a way that makes sense to you. The three-level hierarchy lets you:
- Group related items together
- See the big picture and the details
- Customize your tracking structure

### 2. Flexible Measurement

Not all deeds are measured the same way. Kitaab supports:
- **Scales**: Choose from predefined options (Yes/No, Excellent/Good/Fair, etc.)
- **Counts**: Enter numeric values (pages read, minutes exercised, amount donated)

### 3. Daily Entry Recording

- Record one entry per deed item per day
- Entries are tied to specific dates, so you can track your progress over time
- See your history and patterns

### 4. Entry History

Every change you make is tracked, so you can:
- See when you created or updated an entry
- Review your history
- Understand your patterns over time

### 5. Social Features (Optional)

**Connections**: Connect with trusted friends, family members, or mentors

**Permissions**: Control what others can see and do:
- **Read Permission**: They can see your entries (like a mentor checking in)
- **Write Permission**: They can record entries for you (like a family member helping you track)

**Unidirectional**: You control who sees your data. If you connect with someone, they can see what you share, but you don't automatically see theirs (unless they also connect with you).

### 6. Progress Tracking

**Merits**: Deed-specific achievements
- Example: "Pray Fajr on time for 30 consecutive days"
- Example: "Read 100 pages this month"
- Can use AND logic (all conditions must be met) or OR logic (any condition can fulfill it)

**Targets**: Multi-deed goals
- Example: "Complete 20 Sunnat prayers across all prayer types this month"
- Example: "Exercise 150 minutes per week across all exercise types"

### 7. Daily Reflections

Write daily reflection messages:
- One reflection for Hasanaat (good deeds)
- One reflection for Saiyyiaat (areas for improvement)
- Helps you process your day and set intentions

### 8. Notifications

Set daily reminders:
- Choose your preferred time
- Timezone-aware (works correctly no matter where you are)
- Gentle reminders to record your entries

### 9. Support Messages

In-app messaging system for:
- Getting help with the app
- Reporting issues
- Asking questions

## Social Features

### Why Share?

Sharing your journey with others can:
- Increase accountability
- Provide motivation
- Allow mentors to guide you
- Help family members support you

### How Connections Work

1. **Send a Connection Request**: You request to connect with someone (like a friend or mentor)

2. **They Accept**: They accept your request

3. **Grant Permissions**: You decide what they can see and do:
   - **Read Only**: They can view your entries (like a mentor checking in)
   - **Write Access**: They can record entries for you (like a family member helping you track)

4. **Granular Control**: You share specific deed items, not everything. For example:
   - Share "Daily Prayers" with your mentor
   - Share "Exercise" with your workout partner
   - Keep "Personal Reflections" private

### Privacy First

- You control who sees what
- Connections are one-way by default (you see theirs only if they also connect with you)
- You can revoke permissions at any time
- You can block users if needed

## Progress Tracking

### Merits (Deed-Specific Achievements)

**What are Merits?**
Merits are achievements tied to specific deeds. They help you set and track goals.

**Example Merit:**
- **Title**: "Pray Fajr on Time for 30 Days"
- **Deed Item**: "Fajr Prayer"
- **Target**: "Prayed on time" for 30 consecutive days
- **Type**: AND (all days must meet the condition)

**How It Works:**
1. Create a merit with your goal
2. Define the conditions (what counts as success)
3. The system tracks your progress automatically
4. When you complete it, you get notified

### Targets (Multi-Deed Goals)

**What are Targets?**
Targets are broader goals that span multiple deeds.

**Example Target:**
- **Title**: "Complete 20 Sunnat Prayers This Month"
- **Items**: 
  - 5 Fajr Sunnat prayers
  - 5 Dhuhr Sunnat prayers
  - 5 Maghrib Sunnat prayers
  - 5 Isha Sunnat prayers

**How It Works:**
1. Create a target with your overall goal
2. Break it down into specific items across different deeds
3. Track progress across all items
4. Complete when all conditions are met

## Privacy & Security

### Your Data, Your Control

**Client-Side Encryption** (Advanced Privacy Feature):
- Sensitive data (like deed names, descriptions, reflections) can be encrypted on your device before being stored
- Even the server cannot read your encrypted data without your password
- This means maximum privacy for your personal reflections and sensitive information

**What This Means for You:**
- Your personal reflections are truly private
- Deed names and descriptions can be encrypted
- Only you (and people you explicitly share with) can read your data

### Standard Security

- Secure password storage (your password is never stored in plain text)
- Email verification to ensure account security
- Two-factor authentication support (optional extra security layer)
- Secure connections (all data transmitted securely)

### Data Sharing

When you share data with someone:
- They can only see what you explicitly share
- You control read vs. write permissions
- You can revoke access at any time
- The same data can be shared with multiple people, each with their own access

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is Kitaab only for Muslims?**
A: While the terminology (Hasanaat, Saiyyiaat) comes from Islamic tradition, the concept of tracking good deeds and areas for improvement is universal. Anyone can use Kitaab to track their moral and spiritual development.

**Q: Do I have to record entries every day?**
A: No, but consistency helps you see patterns and make progress. You can record entries whenever you want, but daily recording is recommended for best results.

**Q: Can I change my deed structure later?**
A: Yes! You can reorganize, add items, remove items, and update scales. Your old entries will remain valid.

**Q: What if I miss a day?**
A: That's okay! You can always go back and record entries for past days, or just continue from today. The goal is progress, not perfection.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Do I need an internet connection?**
A: You'll need internet to sync your data, but you can record entries offline and they'll sync when you're back online.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes, you can export your data at any time for backup or analysis.

**Q: What happens if I forget my password?**
A: You can reset your password using the "Forgot Password" feature. If you use encryption, you'll need to re-encrypt your data with your new password.

**Q: Can I use Kitaab on multiple devices?**
A: Yes! Your data syncs across all your devices (phone, tablet, computer).

### Privacy Questions

**Q: Who can see my data?**
A: Only you, unless you explicitly share it with someone. Even with sharing, you control exactly what they can see.

**Q: Is my data encrypted?**
A: Yes, sensitive data can be encrypted on your device before storage. This is optional but recommended for maximum privacy.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Yes, you can delete your account at any time. All your data will be permanently deleted.

**Q: What if I share data with someone and then want to revoke access?**
A: You can revoke permissions at any time. They'll immediately lose access to your shared data.

### Feature Questions

**Q: How many deeds can I create?**
A: As many as you want! There's no limit.

**Q: Can I have multiple scales for the same deed?**
A: Yes, you can create different versions of scales over time. Old entries will still reference the old scale version.

**Q: How do merits and targets differ?**
A: Merits are tied to a single deed item (e.g., "Pray Fajr on time for 30 days"). Targets span multiple deed items (e.g., "Complete 20 prayers across all types this month").

**Q: Can someone else create entries for me?**
A: Yes, if you grant them write permission for specific deed items. This is useful if a family member or mentor is helping you track.

**Q: What's the difference between read and write permissions?**
A: Read permission lets them see your entries. Write permission lets them create or update entries for you.

## Tips for Success

### 1. Start Simple

Don't try to track everything at once. Start with 2-3 important deeds and build from there.

### 2. Be Consistent

Recording entries daily (even if brief) is more valuable than detailed entries once a week.

### 3. Review Regularly

Set aside time weekly or monthly to review your progress, identify patterns, and adjust your goals.

### 4. Use Reflections

Daily reflections help you process your day and set intentions. They're as important as the entries themselves.

### 5. Celebrate Progress

When you complete a merit or target, take a moment to acknowledge your achievement. Progress, not perfection, is the goal.

### 6. Adjust as Needed

Your needs will change over time. Don't be afraid to reorganize your deeds, update your scales, or change your goals.

### 7. Use Social Features Wisely

Sharing with a trusted friend or mentor can increase accountability, but only share what you're comfortable with.
